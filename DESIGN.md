## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the living connection between agroecological sustainability and cultural preservation across Vietnam's heritage rural landscapes. Drawing aesthetic balance from modern editorial publication design and indigenous natural materials, it replaces generic commercial eco-tourism layouts with a curated, quiet, and grounded digital sanctuary.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Rooted & Authentic:** Evoking ancient rice terraces, highland forests, handcrafted bamboo architecture, and tactile rice paper textures.
- **Thoughtful & Curated:** Providing generous breathing room that respects the rhythm of indigenous storytelling and prevents sensory overload.
- **Warm & Welcoming:** Embracing agricultural stewards, researchers, and conscientious eco-travelers with hospitality and reverence.

### Design Movement: Organic Modern Editorial
The design movement blends high-end editorial clarity with organic, tactile minimalism. Content is structured with clean vertical hierarchy, generous white (rice paper) space, refined micro-borders reminiscent of traditional joinery, and poetic, authoritative typography that lends permanence to agricultural lore and heritage discovery.

## Colors

The palette takes direct inspiration from indigenous agricultural ecosystems—mist over limestone karst, terraced fields at harvest, and unbleached handmade mulberry bark paper (*giấy dó*).

### Functional Color Applications
- **Primary (`#3E5433` - Moss Green):** Acts as the foundational voice of brand governance, used for key interactive highlights, top navigation banners, structural header panels, and primary interactive elements.
- **Secondary (`#D99B26` - Earth Gold / Amber):** Represents golden harvests, sunlight, and artisanal craft. Deployed selectively for impactful calls to action, badges, accents, metrics counters, and rating glyphs.
- **Tertiary (`#2B3D23` - Deep Highland Forest):** Grounding contrast tone used for solid footers, immersive hero wraps, and high-impact structural divides.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Soft Rice Paper):** Serves as the primary canvas tint, eliminating stark digital white glare and providing an organic reading bed. Supported by `#F4EFEA` for alternating card sections and recessed UI surfaces.
- **Text & Contrast Hierarchy:** Primary textual content relies on `#1F241D` (Charcoal Ink) rather than pure black, ensuring humanized contrast and eye comfort during long editorial reading. Secondary descriptions and meta tags use `#4B5547`.

## Typography

The typographical architecture combines the timeless, literary dignity of `Noto Serif` with the modern, humanized legibility of `Plus Jakarta Sans`. 

### Typographic Principles
- **Headlines (`Noto Serif`):** Conveys historical gravity, conservation ethics, and agro-cultural narrative. Used for section titles, pull quotes, narrative lead-ins, and hero statements. Vietnamese diacritics are balanced with generous line heights to preserve glyph clarity.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its open aperture, neutral geometric undertone, and clean screen rendering at dense information points (tour logistics, itinerary timings, and booking metadata).
- **Categorical Kicker Labels:** Rendered in small, tracked-out uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` with `letterSpacing: 0.08em` in Earth Gold or Moss Green above major editorial titles to provide contextual categorization without visual weight.
- **Generous Text Leading:** Body copy enforces a 1.6x–1.7x line-height ratio to prevent visual fatigue and facilitate tranquil long-form immersion.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric, editorial 12-column grid system bounded inside a maximum content enclosure of `1280px` to maintain optimal line measures.

### Grid & Structural Rhythm
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) margins. Large narrative sections alternate between 7/5-column split arrangements (image and prose) and 3-column curated listing cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Tour and experience cards collapse from 4-up to 2-up grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) margins. Cards stack cleanly or enter horizontal swipe trails with visible partial overflow to prompt discovery.

### Spacing Narrative
Generous vertical spacing (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) between programmatic clusters creates natural pauses between agricultural tours, impact metrics, village stories, and culinary experiences. Visual breathing room is preserved around photography, allowing images of highland mist and village life to resonate without competing with decorative clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic heavy dropshadows or glossy plastic skeuomorphism, embracing tactile organic layering reminiscent of rice paper, botanical specimens, and architectural wood framing.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas (Level 0):** Unbleached rice paper base (`#FAF8F5`).
- **Surface Layer (Level 1):** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted paper (`#F4EFEA`) surfaces, framed by a soft, low-contrast hairline perimeter (`1px solid #E5DED5`). Used for village accommodation cards, blog posts, and itinerary containers.
- **Elevated Interactive (Level 2):** Applied solely during user focus, active popovers, and card hover states. Achieved via an ambient, warm-tinted shadow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(31, 36, 29, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 36, 29, 0.04)`.
- **High-Contrast Solid Inset (Level 3):** Modal overlays and navigation flyouts sit upon a semi-translucent charcoal moss veil (`rgba(31, 36, 29, 0.6)`), using `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` to maintain environmental grounding behind the foreground panel.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural soft geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Radii are deliberate and understated, echoing traditional wooden timber joints, hand-carved stone signs, and classic bookbinding rather than bubbly consumer software.

### Corner Radius System
- **Standard UI Elements (`4px` / `0.25rem`):** Applied to buttons, form inputs, badge containers, and contextual chips. Keeps interfaces sharp, literary, and grounded.
- **Structural Cards (`8px` / `0.5rem`):** Used on tour cards, image containers, and experiential story previews.
- **Feature Imagery & Media:** Photography maintains soft `8px` rounded corners, accompanied by an optional inner `1px` subtle inset line (`rgba(31, 36, 29, 0.06)`) to preserve clean edge definition against varied image luminosity.
- **Pill Badges (`9999px`):** Reserved strictly for seasonal status badges (e.g., "Mùa Gặt", "Còn 3 Chỗ") to provide immediate semantic differentiation from structural cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action Button:** Solid Deep Moss Green (`#3E5433`) background with pure white typography, `12px 24px` padding, `4px` border radius, and `label-btn` typographic weight. On hover, background deepens to `#2B3D23` with a subtle `translateY(-1px)` shift.
- **Secondary / Accent Button:** Amber Gold (`#D99B26`) fill with `#1F241D` text for high-priority bookings or seasonal calls-to-action.
- **Outline Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#3E5433`, text in `#3E5433`. Hover transitions to `#F4EFEA` background tint.
- **Text Link:** Styled with an underline offset of `4px` in `#D99B26`, maintaining an intentional editorial look with transition effects on hover.

### Heritage Cards (Tours & Stays)
- **Structure:** Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) card on rice paper background with a `1px` border in `#E5DED5`.
- **Imagery:** Aspect ratio locked to `4:3` (tours) or `16:10` (homestays). Images utilize natural warm color temperature without artificial saturation.
- **Content Hierarchy:** 
  1. Small uppercase category kicker in `#C7821B` (e.g., "CHIỀNG YÊN • TREKKING").
  2. Headline in `Noto Serif`, 18px–20px, `#1F241D`.
  3. Detail row (duration, group size, origin village) in `body-sm` `#4B5547` with discrete leaf/dot dividers.
  4. Footer divider with clear price formatting (`690.000đ / người`) and a minimalist right-aligned action arrow.

### Category Chips & Filters
- **Resting:** Rice paper tint (`#F4EFEA`) with `#4B5547` text and hairline border `#E5DED5`.
- **Selected:** Deep Moss (`#3E5433`) fill with white text, providing immediate, authoritative feedback for eco-tour category filtering.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Generous padding (`14px 16px`), `4px` corner radius, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D5CFC7`. Focused state transitions border to `#3E5433` accompanied by a soft `3px` focus ring in `rgba(62, 84, 51, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Customized with `#3E5433` active fill and clean white check mark icons; unchecked state features a clean `#D5CFC7` boundary with no synthetic gloss.

### Impact Metrics Counter
- Numeric highlights displayed in massive `headline-lg` (`Noto Serif`) in Harvest Amber (`#D99B26`), accompanied by compact two-line explanatory captions in `#4B5547` underneath, grounding the network's conservation and community support statistics in a clear, modern format.
